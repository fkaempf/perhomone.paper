INTERPRETATION
--------------
Both pheromone channels (ppk23, ppk25) share the GABA sign-inverter layer
as a common inhibitory relay. The Sankey makes two points visual:
(1) both sources converge onto the GABA SI pool -- the dark-purple ribbons
enter the middle stratum from both ppk23 and ppk25 and spread across
multiple GABAergic cell types rather than concentrating on a single node;
(2) from the GABA SI layer the flow diverges onto a SPECIFIC SUBSET of
mAL_m subtypes rather than spreading uniformly. This selective fan-out is
the circuit substrate that converts a shared excitatory sensory drive into
a subtype-specific inhibitory signature across a distributed GABAergic
pool, producing the differential ppk23 vs ppk25 responses and the E/I
balance shifts seen in earlier panels.
