PANEL M v2: AN05B035 morphology with ppk23/ppk25 channel-resolved synapses
===========================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Four 2D projections of the AN05B035 skeleton (bodyids 23513, 517601). Top row: input
synapses. Bottom row: output synapses. Left column: frontal (XY) view.
Right column: dorsal (XZ) view.

Each synapse is coloured by one of 5+1 discrete categories (not a blended
bias gradient):
  ppk23-sensillum      : partner type in channel_neurons$ppk23
  ppk25-sensillum      : partner type in channel_neurons$ppk25
  relay-ppk23          : AN09B017 variant with log2(ppk23/ppk25) > 1
  relay-ppk25          : AN09B017 variant with log2(ppk25/ppk23) > 1
  mAL_m (ppk23-biased) : output target whose mal_activation_matrix score
                         is higher for ppk23 than ppk25
  mAL_m (ppk25-biased) : output target biased to ppk25
  other                : everything else (interneurons, unlabeled, etc.)

WHY TWO CHANNELS SEPARATED
--------------------------
The previous version compressed ppk23 and ppk25 contributions into one
red-blue bias score per partner type, which collapsed the two channels
at partners that received both. Here the direct sensillum channels are
kept as their own categories and the AN09B017 relay channel is split
by which contact modality dominates its input, so the reader can see
on the neuron where each channel terminates independently.

A Neuroglancer URL is saved to
panel_M_morphology_v2_neuroglancer_url.txt for 3D inspection.
