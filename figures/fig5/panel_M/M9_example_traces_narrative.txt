PANEL CE9: Example ppk23 -> mAL path traces showing AN05B035 as sign inverter
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Three small example networks, one per mAL_m subtype. For each subtype we draw
the top 10 strongest ppk23 -> mAL paths (from `mal_paths`, K=50 per channel).
Nodes are placed at their hop level along the x-axis (source ppk23 sensory
neuron on the left, mAL target on the right); within each hop we stagger in
y to reduce edge crossings. Edges are colored by route -- red when the path
does NOT traverse AN05B035 (direct / AN09B017-type excitatory relays) and
blue when AN05B035 appears anywhere in the path (GABAergic sign-inverter).
Edge width scales with path strength; node size scales with how many of the
top-10 paths pass through that node.

CHOSEN EXAMPLES
---------------
* Most ppk23-INHIBITED:  mAL_m2b  (E/I = +0.11, AN05B035 fraction = 19%)
* Balanced:              mAL_m10  (E/I = +0.04, AN05B035 fraction = 10%)
* Most ppk23-EXCITED:    mAL_m3c  (E/I = +0.62, AN05B035 fraction = 4%)

These three subtypes were picked automatically from `mal_paths`/`mal_strength_*`
to span the extremes and midpoint of ppk23 E/I balance among male-specific
mAL subtypes.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Visualizing only the top-10 paths per target makes the routing asymmetry
explicit: the ppk23-inhibited subtype's trace is dominated by BLUE edges
that converge on a central AN05B035 node before reaching the mAL target,
while the ppk23-excited subtype's trace is almost entirely RED, with paths
sidestepping AN05B035 and flowing through direct / AN09B017-type excitatory
relays. The balanced subtype shows an intermediate mix. This is the
concrete per-subtype picture of the sign inversion that panels C and E
summarize in aggregate.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel CE9 is the mechanistic 'zoom-in' companion to CE1's aggregate routing
fractions and CE2's AN05B035-fraction vs E/I regression: it shows WHICH
paths carry ppk23 drive into each of the three example mALs, and where in
that path the sign inversion happens.
