PANEL K: AN09B017 Output Specificity -- Relay-to-mAL Targeting
=================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Heatmap: Input-normalized weights from each AN09B017 variant (+ AN05B035)
to each mAL type. Values represent the fraction of each mAL neuron's total
input that comes from each relay neuron. Rows clustered by output similarity;
columns clustered by input similarity and annotated by dimorphism
(male-specific vs shared).

Entropy: Shannon entropy of each relay neuron's output distribution across
mAL types. High entropy = broad targeting; low entropy = specific targeting.

Dimorphism targeting: Stacked bar chart showing what fraction of each relay
neuron's output goes to male-specific vs shared mAL types.

Dot plot: Individual connections colored by relay input selectivity.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. Most specific relay: AN09B017a (entropy target: 12 mAL types).
   Most broad relay:    AN09B017d (16 mAL targets).

2. Dimorphism bias: AN09B017a sends 100% of its output to male-specific mAL types;
   AN05B035 sends 51% to shared mAL types.

3. The mAL population includes 15 male-specific and 22 shared types.
   Relay targeting may differ between these dimorphism classes.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
Panel B showed that AN09B017 variants have different ppk23/ppk25 INPUT
selectivity. This panel shows they also differ in OUTPUT specificity --
each variant preferentially innervates different subsets of mAL types.

Combined with the input selectivity spectrum, this creates a mapping:
specific pheromone channels -> specific AN09B017 variants -> specific
mAL types. The relay layer is not a simple pass-through but a
selectivity-amplifying transform that routes pheromone information to
distinct mAL subpopulations.

AN05B035 (GABAergic sign-inverter) adds a qualitatively different
output pattern: it specifically targets the mAL types that show ppk23
sign reversal (Panel C), providing the inhibitory counterweight.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel K complements Panel B by completing the relay neuron story:
- Panel B: relay INPUT selectivity (which pheromone channels drive each variant)
- Panel K: relay OUTPUT specificity (which mAL types each variant targets)

Together they show that the AN09B017 layer implements a labeled-line-like
mapping from pheromone channels to mAL subpopulations, with AN05B035
adding the sign-inverting branch.
