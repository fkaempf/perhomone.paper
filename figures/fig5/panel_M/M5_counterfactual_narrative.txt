PANEL CE5 (counterfactual): removing AN05B035 reveals its sign-inverter role
=============================================================================

DESIGN
------
For each mAL subtype, compute the ppk23 E/I balance ((exc - inh)/(exc + inh))
in two scenarios:
  (1) REAL: all cached strongest ppk23 -> mAL paths.
  (2) COUNTERFACTUAL: drop every path whose trajectory traverses AN05B035
      (identified by substring match in mal_paths$path), then recompute the
      same metric on the remaining paths.

The arrow plot shows each subtype as two stacked points (real dark / CF orange)
connected by an arrow pointing real -> counterfactual. The marginal histogram
shows the distribution of per-subtype shifts (CF - real).

KEY NUMBERS
-----------
  ppk23 paths total:                       8000
  ppk23 paths through AN05B035:            949 (11.9%)
  mean delta (E/I balance, CF - real):     +0.1097
  median delta:                            +0.1046
  subtypes pushed toward excitation:       15
  subtypes pushed toward inhibition:       1
  subtypes unaffected (no AN05B035 paths): 0
  largest single shift:                    mAL_m11  (delta = -0.0142)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
A positive mean delta (CF - real > 0) indicates that AN05B035 on average
*suppresses* ppk23-driven excitation in mAL neurons, consistent with its role
as the GABAergic sign-inverting relay. Subtypes with the largest positive
shifts are precisely those for which AN05B035 dominates the inhibitory branch
of the ppk23 pathway; subtypes with near-zero shift do not route ppk23 through
AN05B035 and therefore are insensitive to its removal.
