FIGURE 5: The mAL Population Code for Pheromone Encounter Discrimination
=========================================================================

CENTRAL THESIS
--------------
The medial antennal lobe (mAL) is not a uniform ON/OFF courtship gate.
It is a heterogeneous population that decomposes pheromone contact into
channel-specific streams, computes encounter identity through a mix of
excitatory relay, sign-reversing inhibition, and lateral competition,
and delivers channel-specific gating of P1 courtship command neurons.


ENCOUNTER SCENARIOS
-------------------
Three biologically grounded encounter types (lone male contact excluded
because male pheromone contact always co-occurs with cVA volatile):

  1. Female encounter:  ppk23 + ppk25     (contact with female)
  2. cVA + male:        DA1 + ppk23       (volatile + male contact)
  3. cVA + female:      DA1 + ppk23 + ppk25 (volatile + female contact)

Key contrasts:
  - cVA+female vs cVA+male:  sex discrimination when cVA is present
  - cVA+female vs Female:    effect of adding volatile context to female contact
  - cVA+male vs Female:      male contact+volatile vs female contact alone

PANEL-BY-PANEL GUIDE
====================

A. Sensory Input Profiles (heatmap)
-----------------------------------
PURPOSE: Establish the puzzle. Show that mAL subtypes have diverse,
non-uniform input profiles across 7 sensory channels. This contradicts
the classical view of mAL as a single homogeneous cell type. The
hierarchical clustering reveals distinct functional groups: ppk23-biased,
ppk25-biased, olfactory-responsive, and multimodal subtypes.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Without this, the reader assumes mAL = one type.
This panel says "look closer -- there are at least 37 subtypes and they
don't all do the same thing."


B. AN09B017 Relay Selectivity Spectrum
--------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Explain HOW input diversity is created. The ascending neuron
family AN09B017 (variants a-g) tiles a selectivity spectrum from ppk23-
biased to ppk25-biased. AN09B017b receives 12.6x more ppk23 than ppk25
input; AN09B017g receives 16x more ppk25 than ppk23. This relay layer
decomposes a mixed pheromone signal into channel-specific streams before
it even reaches mAL.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: This is the mechanistic origin of mAL diversity.
The relay layer does the first stage of pheromone channel separation.


K. AN09B017 Output Specificity (heatmap)
-----------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show that relay selectivity is paired with output specificity.
AN09B017 variants don't just differ in what they receive -- they differ
in which mAL subtypes they target. This completes the labeled-line
mapping: ppk23 -> AN09B017b -> specific mAL subtypes. Shannon entropy
quantifies how broad vs narrow each relay's targeting is.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Together with B, this establishes a complete
input-relay-target mapping. The relay layer is not a passive pass-through
but an active routing system.


C. ppk23 Sign Reversal (paired bars + scatter)
-----------------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show the key computational surprise. The same pheromone receptor
(ppk23, male contact) produces OPPOSITE effects at different mAL subtypes.
Some subtypes are excited; others are inhibited. This is a circuit-level
computation mediated by the GABAergic interneuron AN05B035, which
intercepts ppk23 signals and re-routes them as inhibition to specific
mAL targets.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: This transforms mAL from a simple relay into a
computing element. A single stimulus (male contact) simultaneously excites
some subtypes and inhibits others, enabling population-level encoding of
encounter identity.


E. Valence Decomposition (E/I balance)
---------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Decompose the net responses from Panel C into their excitatory
and inhibitory components. Every mAL subtype receives BOTH excitatory and
inhibitory paths from ppk23 and ppk25. The net response (Panel C) is the
balance of these opposing forces. ppk25 is almost universally excitatory;
ppk23 has a strong inhibitory component via AN05B035.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Shows the mechanistic substrate for sign reversal.
Also implies plasticity potential: small changes in E/I balance could flip
a subtype's response polarity.


G. Lateral Inhibition Architecture
-----------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show that mAL subtypes don't operate independently -- they
compete through lateral GABAergic inhibition. mAL_m1 is the dominant
inhibitory hub (0.76 total input-normalized output to other mAL types),
followed by mAL_m8 (0.39). This competitive architecture sharpens the
population code by suppressing weakly activated subtypes.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Without lateral inhibition, all subtypes would
respond to all channels. Competition creates winner-take-all dynamics
that sharpen population representations. This is the network-level
mechanism that refines the input-driven diversity from Panels A-E.


F/F'. Population Encounter Signatures (parallel coordinates)
------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show the functional payoff. Under 3 biologically grounded
encounter scenarios (female, cVA+male, cVA+female), the mAL population
produces DISTINCT activation patterns. F uses path-based strengths;
F' uses the signal flow model. Both show that the mAL population code
discriminates encounter type, not just "pheromone present."

Difference profiles show:
  - cVA+female vs cVA+male:  which subtypes carry the sex-discrimination
    signal when volatile context is present (the ppk25 effect)
  - cVA+female vs Female:    which subtypes respond to adding cVA volatile
  - cVA+male vs Female:      pure male-with-volatile vs female contrast

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: This is the functional readout of all the
preceding mechanisms. The diversity (A), relay routing (B,K), sign
reversal (C), E/I balance (E), and lateral competition (G) combine to
produce a population code capable of encounter discrimination.


L/L'. ppk Selectivity Propagation to mAL and P1
-------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show that pheromone selectivity extends beyond mAL to the P1
courtship command neurons using the signal flow model.

  X-axis = ppk23 alone (signal flow net input)
  Y-axis = ppk23 + ppk25 coactivation (signal flow net input)

Points above the diagonal gain drive from ppk25 addition (female contact
enhancement); points on the diagonal respond equally to ppk23 alone and
ppk23+ppk25 together (ppk25 adds nothing). P1_6b, P1_3b, and P1_3c are
most enhanced by ppk25 addition.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Connects the mAL population code to downstream
behavioral output. P1 subtypes don't receive uniform pheromone drive --
some are specifically enhanced by female pheromone (ppk25), suggesting
different P1 subtypes gate different courtship subroutines depending on
whether the encountered fly is male or female.


D/D'. mAL-to-P1 Output Specificity
------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Show that mAL subtypes do not uniformly inhibit all P1 neurons.
D shows the full biclustered heatmap revealing block-diagonal structure;
D' shows total drive per P1 subtype ranked by magnitude. mAL_m8 is the
strongest P1 inhibitor (-0.82), followed by mAL_m1 (-0.70). Strikingly,
mAL_m3b (+0.12) and mAL_m3a (+0.12) are net EXCITATORY to P1.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: The block-diagonal structure means sensory streams
maintain labeled-line separation through to motor output. Different
encounter types preferentially release different P1 subtypes.


I. Model Convergence (path-based vs signal flow)
-------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE: Validate predictions using two independent computational
approaches. Path-based (K strongest anatomical paths) and signal flow
(iterative network propagation with rectified sigmoid) strongly agree
(Spearman rho ~0.7-0.8). Scenario discriminability analysis identifies
which subtypes shift most across the 3 encounter scenarios: mAL_m3c
leads, followed by mAL_m3a and mAL_m2a. The spread scatter plot
(point size = discriminability) reveals that male-specific subtypes
dominate the high-activation, high-discriminability quadrant.

WHY THIS PANEL MATTERS: Convergence of two independent models increases
confidence that the population code is real, not an artifact of one
analytical approach. The discriminability analysis identifies the
specific subtypes most responsible for encoding encounter identity.


EXAMPLE NEURONS TO HIGHLIGHT
=============================

Pick 4-5 as "case studies" that each illustrate a different principle:

1. mAL_m1 (GABAergic, male-specific)
   THE CLASSICAL COURTSHIP GATE
   - The population's master regulator
   - Strongest lateral inhibition hub (0.76 normalized output)
   - Second strongest P1 inhibitor (-0.70)
   - Strongly ppk23-excited: activated by contact pheromone
   - Story: "Contact pheromone activates mAL_m1, which suppresses rival
     mAL subtypes through lateral inhibition AND directly inhibits P1
     courtship neurons. This is the classical courtship-suppressing
     mAL function -- but it's just one subtype in a diverse population."

2. mAL_m3b (unclear NT, male-specific)
   THE SURPRISE EXCITER
   - One of only two mAL types with net EXCITATORY P1 output (+0.12)
   - Challenges the dogma that mAL only inhibits courtship
   - High signal flow activation, moderate scenario discriminability
   - Story: "Not all mAL-to-P1 transmission is inhibitory. mAL_m3b
     is a pro-courtship element within a predominantly inhibitory
     population, suggesting mAL output can PROMOTE specific courtship
     subroutines under certain sensory combinations."

3. mAL_m3c (GABAergic, male-specific)
   THE ENCOUNTER ENCODER
   - Most scenario-discriminating subtype across all 3 encounters
   - Strongest ppk23 excitation (0.11) and highest signal flow response
   - Its activation shifts most between female, cVA+male, cVA+female
   - Story: "mAL_m3c is the most context-sensitive subtype. Its
     activation best distinguishes who the fly is touching and whether
     volatile pheromone is present, making it a candidate encoder of
     encounter identity."

4. mAL4F (glutamatergic, shared)
   THE SIGN-REVERSED DETECTOR
   - Most ppk23-inhibited subtype (strongest sign reversal)
   - Excited by ppk25, inhibited by ppk23
   - Shared (present in both sexes), unlike most active mAL subtypes
   - Story: "Contact with a male SUPPRESSES mAL4F via the AN05B035
     sign-reversing relay, while female contact excites it via ppk25.
     This subtype could signal 'female contact' by responding only
     when ppk25 dominates ppk23. The sign reversal is a circuit-level
     computation, not a receptor property."

5. AN09B017b + AN09B017g (ascending relay neurons)
   THE SELECTIVITY SPECTRUM
   - AN09B017b: ppk23-selective (12.6:1 ratio)
   - AN09B017g: ppk25-selective (0.06 ratio = 16:1 for ppk25)
   - Each targets different mAL subsets (Panel K)
   - Story: "These relay neurons sit at opposite ends of the pheromone
     selectivity spectrum. AN09B017b routes male contact (ppk23) and
     AN09B017g routes female contact (ppk25) to distinct mAL subsets.
     The relay layer is where pheromone discrimination begins -- before
     mAL even performs its own computations."

Optional 6th:
6. P1_6b (P1 courtship command neuron)
   THE FEMALE-SENSITIVE OUTPUT
   - Most enhanced by ppk25 addition (+0.040 delta in signal flow)
   - Drive increases substantially when ppk25 is added to ppk23
   - Story: "P1_6b receives substantially more drive when ppk25 is
     coactivated with ppk23 (female contact) compared to ppk23 alone.
     This makes it the P1 subtype most sensitive to female pheromone,
     and a candidate gate for female-specific courtship behaviors."

============================

streams through differential relay routing, sign-reversing inhibition,
and lateral competition, producing distinct encounter signatures that
gate specific P1 courtship command neurons."
