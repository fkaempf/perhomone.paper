Panel A supplement -- intermediate-node variants collapsed
===========================================================

Collapse rule: for each cached path, strip trailing single lowercase letter
from every *intermediate* node (not the sensory source, not the mAL target).
Example: AN09B017a/b/c/d/e/f/g all collapse to AN09B017.

Paths that become identical after collapsing are deduplicated; their
strengths are summed (biologically: the effective aggregate AN09B017 neuron
contributes the pooled synaptic drive of its variants along the shared
downstream route).
