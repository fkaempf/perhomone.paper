PANEL M: Example ppk25 -> mAL path traces (F-cell channel, GABA sign-inverter pool)
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
F-cell counterpart of the ppk23 example traces. For three mAL_m subtypes we
draw the top 10 strongest ppk25 -> mAL paths. Nodes are placed at their
canonical hop level along x (ppk25 source left, mAL target right); each
unique node appears at exactly ONE (x, y) (canonical hop = most common hop
across top-K paths). Edges are colored by route: red = path does NOT
traverse any GABA sign-inverter; blue = path passes through at least one
member of the GABA sign-inverter pool (AN05B021, AN05B023a, AN05B023b, AN05B023c, AN05B023d, AN05B025, AN05B035, AN05B050_a, IN05B002, IN05B011a, IN05B011b). Edge width scales with path
strength; node size scales with how many of the top-10 paths pass through
that node.

CHOSEN EXAMPLES
---------------
* Most ppk25-INHIBITED:  mAL_m1  (E/I = +0.39, GABA SI fraction = 29%)
* Balanced:              mAL_m2b  (E/I = +0.43, GABA SI fraction = 19%)
* Most ppk25-EXCITED:    mAL_m3b  (E/I = +0.67, GABA SI fraction = 7%)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Together with the ppk23 traces, this panel shows that the GABA sign-inverter
pool sits on the sign-inverting route for BOTH pheromone channels. The
per-subtype distribution of GABA-SI routing differs between ppk23 and ppk25,
which is what produces the channel-specific E/I asymmetries summarized in
panels C and E.
