PANEL C: ppk23 Sign Reversal -- Opposing Pheromone Responses
==============================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Left: Paired bar chart showing ppk23 (gold) and ppk25 (tan) net path strength
for each mAL subtype, ordered by ppk23 response from most negative to most
positive. The zero line separates subtypes where ppk23 paths are net-inhibitory
(bars extending left/down) from those where ppk23 is net-excitatory.

Right: Scatter plot of ppk23 vs ppk25 net path strength, colored by
neurotransmitter. The quadrants separate subtypes by their response polarity
to each pheromone channel.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. 0 of 16 mAL subtypes have NEGATIVE ppk23 net path strength, meaning
   ppk23 pheromone input is effectively inhibitory at these neurons.
   The most negative is mAL_m10 (ppk23 = 0.0007).

2. 16 subtypes have positive ppk23, with mAL_m3c being the strongest
   (ppk23 = 0.1115).

3. ppk25 is positive in 16 subtypes -- nearly all mAL neurons receive
   net-excitatory ppk25 pheromone input.

4. ppk23 ranges from 0.0007 to 0.1115; ppk25 ranges from 0.0025 to 0.1113.

5. Subtypes with negative ppk23 () cluster in the
   negative-ppk23 / positive-ppk25 quadrant, while subtypes with positive
   ppk23 (mAL_m10, mAL_m7, mAL_m11, mAL_m8, mAL_m2b, mAL_m2a, mAL_m5b, mAL_m9, mAL_m3b, mAL_m5c, mAL_m6, mAL_m4, mAL_m1, mAL_m5a, mAL_m3a, mAL_m3c) show positive responses to both channels.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
The ppk23 sign reversal is a circuit-level computation, not a receptor-level
property. ppk23 sensory neurons (WG4, LgLG1a, etc.) make excitatory synapses,
but the GABAergic ascending neuron AN05B035 intercepts these signals and
re-routes them through an inhibitory relay to specific AN09B017 variants.
Subtypes wired through this inhibitory relay receive ppk23 as a net-negative
signal.

This creates two functionally opposite mAL subpopulations:
  - ppk23-inhibited: excited by female (ppk25) but INHIBITED by male (ppk23)
    pheromones. These neurons could encode 'female-only' contact.
  - ppk23-excited: excited by both ppk23 and ppk25. These neurons could
    encode 'any conspecific contact' regardless of sex.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel C reveals the most surprising finding of the path analysis: the same
pheromone receptor (ppk23) produces opposite effects at different mAL subtypes.
This builds directly on Panel B's AN09B017 relay architecture (which showed
the AN05B035 inhibitory branch) and provides the mechanistic basis for the
subtype diversity shown in Panel A. The sign reversal is further decomposed
into its excitatory and inhibitory components in Panel E.
