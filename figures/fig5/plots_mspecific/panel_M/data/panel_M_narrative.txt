INTERPRETATION
--------------
Both pheromone channels (ppk23, ppk25) share the AN05B035 GABAergic
sign-inverter as a common relay. The Sankey makes two points visual:
(1) both sources converge onto AN05B035 -- the dark-purple ribbons enter
the middle stratum from both ppk23 and ppk25;
(2) from AN05B035 the flow diverges onto a SPECIFIC SUBSET of mAL_m
subtypes rather than spreading uniformly. This selective fan-out is the
circuit substrate that converts a shared excitatory sensory drive into a
subtype-specific inhibitory signature, producing the differential ppk23 vs
ppk25 responses and the E/I balance shifts seen in earlier panels.
