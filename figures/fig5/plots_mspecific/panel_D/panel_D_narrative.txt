PANEL D: mAL-to-P1 Output Specificity -- Channel-Specific Gating
===================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Top: A biclustered heatmap of direct synaptic weights from 16 mAL subtypes
(rows) to 45 P1 courtship command neuron subtypes (columns). Color encodes
signed synaptic weight: blue = inhibitory (GABAergic mAL output), white = no
connection, red = excitatory (unclear NT subtypes). Both rows and columns are
hierarchically clustered to reveal block-diagonal structure.

Bottom: Stacked bar chart showing total inhibitory drive from mAL onto each
P1 subtype, decomposed by mAL subtype.

Also: Dot plot showing each mAL->P1 connection as a point sized by weight
and colored by sign.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. 15 of 16 mAL subtypes make direct synapses onto P1 neurons; 32 of 45
   P1 subtypes receive mAL input.

2. The connectivity is NOT uniform: mAL subtypes do not all inhibit the same
   P1 neurons. Instead, different mAL subtypes preferentially target
   different P1 subtypes.

3. The strongest single connection is mAL_m1 -> P1_3c (723 synapses).

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
If all mAL subtypes uniformly inhibited all P1 neurons, then mAL would
function as a simple ON/OFF gate for courtship. Instead, the block-diagonal
structure reveals that mAL implements CHANNEL-SPECIFIC gating: each sensory
stream (carried by a different mAL subtype) can independently modulate
different P1 command neurons.

This means the courtship decision is not binary (go/no-go) but graded --
different P1 subtypes may be released from inhibition by different sensory
combinations, potentially activating different aspects of courtship behavior.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel D closes the sensory-to-motor loop opened by Panel A. While Panel A
shows that mAL subtypes receive different sensory inputs, Panel D shows that
they also SEND different outputs to the courtship command layer. Together,
they establish that the mAL population implements a labeled-line mapping
from sensory channels to motor command channels. This is essential context
for Panel H, which shows that this structured connectivity is sexually
dimorphic.
