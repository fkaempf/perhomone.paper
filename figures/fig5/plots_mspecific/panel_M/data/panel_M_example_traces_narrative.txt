PANEL CE9: Example ppk23 -> mAL path traces showing GABA sign-inverter pool routing
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Three small example networks, one per mAL_m subtype. For each subtype we draw
the top 10 strongest ppk23 -> mAL paths (from `mal_paths`, K=50 per channel).
Nodes are placed at their canonical hop level along the x-axis (source ppk23
sensory neuron on the left, mAL target on the right); within each hop we
stagger in y to reduce edge crossings. Each unique node gets exactly ONE
(x, y) position (canonical hop = most common hop across top-K paths). Edges
are colored by route -- red when the path does NOT traverse any member of
the GABA sign-inverter pool (direct / AN09B017-type excitatory relays) and
blue when the path passes through at least one GABA sign-inverter
(GABA SI pool: AN05B021, AN05B023a, AN05B023b, AN05B023c, AN05B023d, AN05B025, AN05B035, AN05B050_a, IN05B002, IN05B011a, IN05B011b).
Edge width scales with path strength; node size scales with how many of the
top-10 paths pass through that node.

CHOSEN EXAMPLES
---------------
* Most ppk23-INHIBITED:  mAL_m10  (E/I = +0.04, GABA SI fraction = 29%)
* Balanced:              mAL_m8  (E/I = +0.12, GABA SI fraction = 23%)
* Most ppk23-EXCITED:    mAL_m3b  (E/I = +0.40, GABA SI fraction = 13%)

These three subtypes were picked automatically from `mal_paths`/`mal_strength_*`
to span the extremes and midpoint of ppk23 E/I balance among male-specific
mAL subtypes.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Visualizing only the top-10 paths per target makes the routing asymmetry
explicit: the ppk23-inhibited subtype's trace is dominated by BLUE edges
that converge on central GABA-sign-inverter nodes before reaching the mAL
target, while the ppk23-excited subtype's trace is almost entirely RED,
with paths sidestepping the GABA SI pool and flowing through direct /
AN09B017-type excitatory relays. The balanced subtype shows an intermediate
mix. This is the concrete per-subtype picture of the sign inversion that
panels C and E summarize in aggregate.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel CE9 is the mechanistic 'zoom-in' companion to CE1's aggregate routing
fractions and CE2's GABA-SI-fraction vs E/I regression: it shows WHICH
paths carry ppk23 drive into each of the three example mALs, and where in
that path the sign inversion happens.
