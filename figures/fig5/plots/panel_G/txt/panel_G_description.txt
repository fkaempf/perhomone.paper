
Panel G: Lateral inhibition shapes the mAL population code
============================================================
The mAL population is heavily interconnected: GABAergic subtypes inhibit
each other, structuring the population response beyond what sensory inputs
alone would predict.

Top:    mAL-to-mAL signed connectivity heatmap (rows = pre, cols = post).
        Rows/columns annotated by NT and dimorphism.

Middle: Hub analysis -- bar charts of total lateral output (how much each
        subtype inhibits others) and total lateral input received.

Bottom: Cross-group targeting -- do dimorphism groups preferentially
        inhibit within-group or between-group?

Data source: Direct synaptic connectivity from the connectome (conn table),
signed by presynaptic neurotransmitter identity.
