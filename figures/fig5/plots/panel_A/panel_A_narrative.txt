PANEL A: mAL Input Profiles -- Sensory Specialization Heatmap
================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
A diverging heatmap displaying the net sensory input strength (excitatory minus
inhibitory path strength) for each of the 37 mAL interneuron subtypes across
7 sensory channels: three olfactory (DA1, VA1v, VA1d), one auditory, one
visual, and two contact chemosensory (ppk23 and ppk25).

Rows are hierarchically clustered (Ward's method on Euclidean distance) to
reveal groups of subtypes with similar input profiles. Row annotations mark
each subtype's neurotransmitter identity (25 GABAergic, 5 unclear) and dimorphism
(male-specific vs shared). Column annotations mark the sensory modality group.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. mAL subtypes are NOT uniformly driven by all sensory channels. The
   clustering reveals distinct input profile groups.

2. The strongest average channel is ppk25; the weakest is auditory.

3. 11 subtypes show NEGATIVE net ppk23 strength (mAL4B, mAL4D, mAL4E, mAL4F, mAL4G, mAL4H, mAL4I, mAL6, mALB1, mALB4, mALD3), indicating that
   inhibitory paths from ppk23 sensory neurons dominate over excitatory
   paths at these neurons -- the signature of the GABAergic sign-inverting
   relay through AN05B035 (detailed in Panel C).

4. 26 subtypes show positive ppk23 strength (mAL_m1, mAL_m10, mAL_m11, mAL_m2a, mAL_m2b, mAL_m3a, mAL_m3b, mAL_m3c, mAL_m4, mAL_m5a, mAL_m5b, mAL_m5c, mAL_m6, mAL_m7, mAL_m8, mAL_m9, mAL4A, mAL4C, mAL5A1, mAL5A2, mAL5B, mALB2, mALB3, mALB5, mALD1, mALD4), receiving ppk23
   input through excitatory relay pathways.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
The mAL interneuron population, classically treated as a single cell type
that uniformly inhibits P1 courtship command neurons, is in fact a
heterogeneous ensemble of at least 37 functionally distinct subtypes.
Each subtype samples a different combination of sensory modalities, meaning
the mAL population can encode WHICH sensory channels are active, not merely
WHETHER pheromone contact has occurred.

----------------------------
