PANEL M: SI-traversing path contribution vs other paths
========================================================

DESIGN
------
For each (channel, mAL_m) pair, paths in mal_paths (top-50 strongest
from ORN -> mAL_m per pair) are partitioned by whether any interior
node is a GABA sign-inverter pool member. Stacked bars show the
partitioned total strength; the SI fraction is (SI-traversing
strength) / (total strength).

POOL (n=11): AN05B021, AN05B023a-d, AN05B025, AN05B035, AN05B050_a, IN05B002, IN05B011a/b.

CHANNEL-LEVEL SI FRACTION
-------------------------
ppk23: 22.1% of total drive routes via the SI pool (raw strength 0.351)
ppk25: 19.5% of total drive routes via the SI pool (raw strength 0.273)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
A stacked bar heavily blue means the mAL_m subtype gets most of its
channel drive through the SI pool; a mostly-grey bar means SI pool
routing is a minor slice. This complements
panel_M_inhibitory_sources (which restricts to inhibitory paths and
decomposes SI vs mAL-lateral vs other) by instead partitioning
TOTAL drive (excitatory + inhibitory) into SI-traversing vs not.
