
Panel F: The connectome predicts distinct population-level signatures
=====================================================================
3 biologically relevant encounter scenarios produce distinct mAL population
response patterns, computed from path strengths:

1. Female encounter:   ppk23 + ppk25 (contact with female fly)
2. cVA + male:         DA1 + ppk23 (olfactory + contact with male)
3. cVA + female:       DA1 + ppk23 + ppk25 (olfactory + contact with female)

Note: lone male encounter (ppk23 alone) is excluded because male contact
always co-occurs with cVA volatile pheromone.

Left:  Parallel coordinates plot (mAL subtypes as axes, 4 colored lines)
Right: Difference profiles between scenario pairs
