PANEL M: AN05B035 morphology with ppk23/ppk25-biased synapses
==============================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Two 2D projections of the AN05B035 skeleton (both hemispheres,
bodyids 517601, 23513), with input and output synapses plotted as points and
colour-coded by the ppk23/ppk25 contact-pheromone drive of the partner
cell type:
  RED   : partner type is predominantly driven by ppk23 (male cuticular contact)
  BLUE  : partner type is predominantly driven by ppk25 (female cuticular contact)
  GREY  : partner type is balanced or has no detectable ppk23/ppk25 drive

METHOD
------
Skeleton fetched via `neuprintr::neuprint_read_skeletons` (male-cns:v0.9,
healed). Synapses fetched via `neuprint_get_synapses`; prepost=1 -> input
(AN05B035 is postsynaptic), prepost=0 -> output (AN05B035 is presynaptic).
Each partner bodyid is mapped to its cell type via mba, and each type is
scored by bias = (drive_ppk23 - drive_ppk25) / (drive_ppk23 + drive_ppk25)
where drives are total synaptic input from the ppk23 vs ppk25 channel-neuron
sets defined in channel_neurons (fig5 setup.R). |bias| >= 0.2 classifies a
partner as male- or female-biased.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
This panel lets the reader see *spatially* where AN05B035 reads male- vs
female-contact information on its dendrite, and where it projects those
two streams on its axon. Colour clustering of inputs by bias highlights
whether the ppk23/ppk25 segregation is preserved at the level of synapse
locations on AN05B035, not just as bulk connectivity statistics.
