PANEL C: Dual-Channel Sign Reversal at mAL via the GABA sign-inverter pool
==============================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Left: Paired bar chart showing ppk23 (gold) and ppk25 (tan) net path strength
for each mAL subtype, ordered by ppk23 response from most negative to most
positive. The zero line separates subtypes where ppk23 paths are net-inhibitory
(bars extending left/down) from those where ppk23 is net-excitatory.

Right: Scatter plot of ppk23 vs ppk25 net path strength, colored by
neurotransmitter. The quadrants separate subtypes by their response polarity
to each pheromone channel.

UPDATED FINDING (DUAL-CHANNEL GABA SIGN-INVERTER POOL INPUT)
------------------------------------------------------------
Contact-pheromone sign reversal is NOT carried by a single neuron: it is
distributed across an 11-cell GABA sign-inverter POOL (AN05B021, AN05B023a-d,
AN05B025, AN05B035, AN05B050_a, IN05B002, IN05B011a, IN05B011b). AN05B035 is
the highest-throughput member, but its siblings collectively carry a
comparable share of the inhibitory ppk23/ppk25 routing. Pool members receive
direct input from BOTH ppk23 and ppk25 contact sensilla; because the pool is
GABAergic, paths from EITHER channel that pass through any pool member are
sign-inverted before reaching their mAL_m targets.

The supplementary figure alongside this panel (panel_M/pdf/panel_M_C_dual_channel.pdf)
makes this explicit: row (a) ppk23 path drive, row (b) ppk25 path drive, row
(c) the GABA sign-inverter pool's signed contribution to ppk23 drive, row (d)
the same pool's signed contribution to ppk25 drive -- all sharing the same
mAL_m x-axis (sorted by ppk23 path drive). Rows (c) and (d) carry the same
negative-going signature, demonstrating that the pool's inhibitory imprint is
present on BOTH contact channels. Accordingly, sign reversal should be framed
as a property of the distributed GABA sign-inverter bottleneck, not as an
AN05B035-specific or ppk23-specific phenomenon.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. 11 of 37 mAL subtypes have NEGATIVE ppk23 net path strength, meaning
   ppk23 pheromone input is effectively inhibitory at these neurons.
   The most negative is mAL4F (ppk23 = -0.0011).

2. 26 subtypes have positive ppk23, with mAL_m3c being the strongest
   (ppk23 = 0.1115).

3. ppk25 is positive in 26 subtypes -- nearly all mAL neurons receive
   net-excitatory ppk25 pheromone input.

4. ppk23 ranges from -0.0011 to 0.1115; ppk25 ranges from -0.0011 to 0.1113.

5. Subtypes with negative ppk23 (mAL4F, mAL4D, mAL4B, mAL4E, mALB4, mAL6, mAL4I, mAL4G, mAL4H, mALD3, mALB1) cluster in the
   negative-ppk23 / positive-ppk25 quadrant, while subtypes with positive
   ppk23 (mALB3, mALD1, mALB2, mALD4, mAL4A, mALB5, mAL_m10, mAL4C, mAL_m7, mAL_m11, mAL_m8, mAL_m2b, mAL_m2a, mAL_m5b, mAL5B, mAL_m9, mAL_m3b, mAL5A1, mAL_m5c, mAL_m6, mAL_m4, mAL_m1, mAL_m5a, mAL_m3a, mAL5A2, mAL_m3c) show positive responses to both channels.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
The ppk23 sign reversal is a circuit-level computation, not a receptor-level
property. ppk23 sensory neurons (WG4, LgLG1a, etc.) make excitatory synapses,
but a distributed pool of GABAergic ascending/interneurons (the GABA
sign-inverter pool: AN05B021, AN05B023a-d, AN05B025, AN05B035, AN05B050_a,
IN05B002, IN05B011a, IN05B011b) intercepts these signals and re-routes them
through an inhibitory relay to specific AN09B017 variants. Subtypes wired
through this inhibitory relay receive ppk23 as a net-negative signal.

This creates two functionally opposite mAL subpopulations:
  - ppk23-inhibited: excited by female (ppk25) but INHIBITED by male (ppk23)
    pheromones. These neurons could encode 'female-only' contact.
  - ppk23-excited: excited by both ppk23 and ppk25. These neurons could
    encode 'any conspecific contact' regardless of sex.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel C reveals the most surprising finding of the path analysis: the same
pheromone receptor (ppk23) produces opposite effects at different mAL subtypes.
This builds directly on Panel B's AN09B017 relay architecture (which showed
the GABA sign-inverter inhibitory branch) and provides the mechanistic basis
for the subtype diversity shown in Panel A. The sign reversal is further
decomposed into its excitatory and inhibitory components in Panel E.
