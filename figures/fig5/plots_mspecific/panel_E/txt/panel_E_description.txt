
Panel E: Excitatory and inhibitory paths create distinct ppk response profiles
=================================================================================
For each mAL subtype, the ppk23 and ppk25 path strengths decompose into
excitatory and inhibitory components via different relay neuron pathways.

Left:  Stacked bar chart showing excitatory (red) and inhibitory (blue)
       path strength for ppk23 and ppk25 per mAL subtype.

Right: Scatter of ppk23 E/I balance vs ppk25 E/I balance. Subtypes with
       strong ppk23 inhibition use the GABAergic AN05B035 relay.

Key finding: The sign reversal of ppk23 at certain subtypes is driven
by stronger inhibitory than excitatory path strengths, reflecting the
GABAergic sign-inverting relay through AN05B035.
