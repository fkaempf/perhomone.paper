
Panel I: Two independent models converge on the same population predictions
============================================================================
The path-based analysis (Panel F) and signal flow model make independent
predictions about how mAL subtypes respond to pheromone encounter scenarios.
This panel compares their predictions to assess robustness.

Left:  Signal flow scenario heatmap (3 scenarios x mAL subtypes)
Right: Scatter comparing path-based vs signal flow activation per subtype,
       faceted by scenario.

Key finding: The two models agree on the major features of the population
code, particularly which subtypes are activated vs inhibited by each scenario.
