PANEL E: Valence Decomposition -- Excitatory vs Inhibitory Path Balance
=========================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Left: Stacked bar chart showing the excitatory (red, upward) and inhibitory
(blue, downward) path strength components for ppk23 (top facet) and ppk25
(bottom facet) at each mAL subtype. The NET strength (bar tips) equals
excitatory minus inhibitory -- i.e., what Panel A and Panel C display as
the activation value. Here we decompose that net value into its two
underlying components.

Right: E/I balance scatter plot showing each subtype's ppk23 E/I ratio
(x-axis) vs ppk25 E/I ratio (y-axis), where E/I = (exc - inh)/(exc + inh).
Values near +1 indicate purely excitatory paths; values near -1 indicate
purely inhibitory paths.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. For ppk23: 0 of 16 subtypes have inhibitory paths stronger than
   excitatory paths, explaining the negative ppk23 values in Panel C.

2. For ppk25: only 0 subtypes have dominant inhibitory paths -- ppk25
   is overwhelmingly excitatory across the mAL population.

3. The subtype receiving the strongest ppk23 inhibition is mAL_m5a; the
   strongest ppk23 excitation goes to mAL_m3c.

4. Mean ppk23 E/I balance across all subtypes = 0.314; mean ppk25 E/I
   balance = 0.424.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
Every mAL subtype receives BOTH excitatory and inhibitory paths from ppk23
and ppk25 sensory neurons. The net response (positive or negative) emerges
from the balance of these opposing forces. This means the sign reversal at
certain subtypes is not because they lack excitatory ppk23 paths, but
because the inhibitory paths (through the AN05B035 GABAergic relay) are
STRONGER than the excitatory ones.

This dual-path architecture provides the circuit with robustness: small
changes in synaptic weight at the relay layer could shift subtypes between
response profiles, potentially enabling experience-dependent plasticity in
sex discrimination.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel E provides the mechanistic depth beneath Panel C's sign-reversal
observation. While Panel C shows the net effect (positive vs negative ppk23),
Panel E shows WHY: the competition between excitatory and inhibitory paths.
This connects to Panel B (which identifies the relay neurons carrying each
pathway) and to Panel G (which shows how mAL_m2a specifically integrates
convergent excitation and lateral inhibition).
