P1 scenario-decoder
====================
For each of 45 P1 courtship subtypes (and 20 P1-level-1 groups), drive under
the three pheromone-encounter scenarios was extracted from the signal-flow
model (p1_all_combos$net_input). For each row the 3-scenario vector was
z-scored, so the question reduces to 'which scenario stands out, regardless
of overall drive magnitude'.
