PANEL M (supplementary): GABA-sign-inverter-attributable inhibition, absolute strength
=====================================================================================

Companion to the primary figure panel_M_inhibitory_sources. That figure shows
share (normalised to 100%); this supplement shows absolute summed path
strength on each channel so subtype magnitude is also readable.

For each of the 16 male-specific mAL_m subtypes we compute the total
inhibitory path strength arriving from ppk23 and ppk25 (rows of
mal_strength_inh), and the subset of that strength carried by paths whose
interior nodes contain ANY member of the GABA sign-inverter pool
(gaba_sign_inverter_types, 11 AN05B / IN05 GABA relay types).

MEAN GABA SI SHARE:
  ppk23: 57.7%
  ppk25: 59.6%

Palette and row order are inherited from the primary figure.
