PANEL B: The AN09B017 Relay -- Ascending Neuron Selectivity Spectrum
======================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Left: Scatter plot of ppk23 vs ppk25 direct synaptic input (synapse count)
to each of the 7 AN09B017 ascending neuron variants (a through g). Points
are colored by dominant input: ppk23-biased (gold), ppk25-biased (tan), or
balanced (grey). A diagonal line marks equal sensitivity.

Right: Log2 ratio bar chart (ppk23/ppk25 input ratio) for each variant,
quantifying the degree of selectivity.

Additionally, a 3-layer bipartite network diagram shows the full
ppk23/ppk25 sensory -> AN09B017 -> mAL connectivity architecture.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. The 7 AN09B017 variants tile a selectivity spectrum: 5 are ppk23-biased,
   1 are ppk25-biased, and 1 are balanced.

2. AN09B017a receives the most ppk23 input; AN09B017g receives the most ppk25 input.

3. AN09B017 variants collectively contact 17 mAL subtypes through 93
   individual connections, distributing sensory information non-uniformly
   across the mAL population.

4. 7 connections from AN05B035 (GABAergic) to AN09B017 variants provide
   sign-inverting inhibition, which is the circuit basis for the ppk23
   sign reversal described in Panel C.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
AN09B017 is not a single relay neuron but a family of variants with graded
ppk23/ppk25 selectivity. This means the ascending neuron layer does not
simply forward pheromone signals -- it decomposes the signal into a spectrum
of channel-specific streams. Each AN09B017 variant preferentially routes
one pheromone channel's information to specific mAL subtypes downstream.

The GABAergic AN05B035 sign-inverter adds a critical inhibitory branch:
ppk23 signals reaching AN09B017g through this pathway arrive with flipped
sign, creating the substrate for the F-selective mAL class.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel B explains HOW the sensory selectivity shown in Panel A is created.
The AN09B017 relay layer is the circuit mechanism that maps continuous
pheromone receptor activation into discrete mAL subtype channels. It sets
up Panel C's sign-reversal finding by showing the AN05B035 inhibitory
branch and connects to Panel G's mAL_m2a case study by showing which
AN09B017 variants provide its excitatory input.
