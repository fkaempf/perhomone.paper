PANEL J: ppk23 Response Group vs P1 Connectivity
===================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
mAL subtypes are split into two groups based on their response to ppk23
(male contact): ppk23-inhibited (11 subtypes, net negative path strength)
and ppk23-excited (26 subtypes, net positive). This panel asks: do these
two groups connect differently to downstream P1 courtship command neurons?

Connectivity is measured as INPUT-NORMALIZED synaptic weights -- the fraction
of each P1 neuron's total input that comes from each mAL subtype. This
controls for differences in P1 neuron size and total synapse count.

Top: Heatmap of signed input-normalized mAL -> P1 weights, with rows split
by ppk23 response group and annotated by NT.

Middle: Box plot comparing total P1 output between the two groups.

Bottom: Per-P1 bar chart showing the total input fraction from each group.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
If ppk23-inhibited subtypes (which are suppressed during male contact)
connect more strongly to P1, this creates a disinhibition mechanism:
male pheromones suppress these mAL subtypes, releasing P1 from their
inhibitory drive. Conversely, ppk23-excited subtypes (activated during
male contact) would INCREASE P1 inhibition during male encounters.

The differential targeting of P1 subtypes by the two groups means that
male vs female contact could selectively release different P1 command
neurons from inhibition, enabling sex-specific courtship responses.

PLACE IN THE FIGURE 5 STORY
----------------------------
Panel J connects the ppk23 sign reversal (Panel C) to the P1 output
specificity (Panel D). While Panel C showed that subtypes differ in
ppk23 polarity and Panel D showed they target different P1 neurons,
Panel J tests whether these two properties are CORRELATED -- whether
the subtypes that are inhibited by male contact are the same ones that
project most heavily to specific P1 command neurons.
