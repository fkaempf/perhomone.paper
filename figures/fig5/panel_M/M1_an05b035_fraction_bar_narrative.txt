PANEL CE1: AN05B035 as the GABAergic sign-inverter in the ppk23 -> mAL pathway
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Horizontal stacked bars (one per male-specific mAL_m subtype) partitioning
total ppk23 -> mAL path strength into two routes:
  BLUE  : paths whose intermediate nodes contain AN05B035 (GABAergic relay)
  RED   : all other paths (direct, or via AN09B017 a-e / other excitatory relays)
Bars are normalized to 100% and rows sorted by AN05B035 fraction (top = highest).

METHOD
------
For each ppk23 -> mAL_m path (K=50 strongest paths per channel, loaded via
mal_paths / setup_mspecific.R), we check whether 'AN05B035' appears anywhere
in the path string. Path strength is summed separately for each route per mAL.

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
- 0 / 16 mAL_m subtypes are AN05B035-dominant (inh route >= 50%):
    
- 9 / 16 mAL_m subtypes are AN09B017-dominant (inh route <= 10%):
    mAL_m3c, mAL_m4, mAL_m3a, mAL_m11, mAL_m5c
- 7 mAL_m subtypes show mixed routing.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Panel C showed that ppk23 produces net-negative drive at a specific subset of
mAL_m subtypes. This panel mechanistically pins that sign reversal on a single
ascending neuron: AN05B035. mALs that sit in the AN05B035-dominant group inherit
ppk23 signals almost entirely through the GABAergic relay, accounting for their
negative ppk23 response in panel C. mALs in the AN09B017-dominant group receive
ppk23 through the parallel excitatory relay, matching their positive ppk23
response. This bar chart is therefore the mechanistic bridge between panel C
(sign-reversal outcome) and panel E (E/I decomposition).
