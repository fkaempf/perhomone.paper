PANEL L: ppk23 vs ppk25 Selectivity — mAL and P1
===================================================

WHAT THIS PANEL SHOWS
---------------------
Three scatter plots comparing ppk23 vs ppk25 drive to mAL and P1 subtypes:
1) Path-based net drive (exc - inh path strength) for mAL subtypes
2) Signal flow model net input for mAL subtypes
3) Signal flow model net input for P1 courtship command neurons

KEY OBSERVATIONS
----------------
1. Path-based mAL: of 16 subtypes, 16 receive detectable ppk input.
   Selectivity: 3 ppk23-biased, 6 ppk25-biased, 7 balanced, 0 none.

2. Signal flow mAL: 16 / 16 subtypes show detectable ppk drive.
   Models largely agree on which subtypes are ppk-responsive.

3. Signal flow P1: 45 / 45 P1 subtypes receive detectable ppk drive.
   P1 subtypes show how pheromone signals reach courtship command neurons
   after propagating through the network (including via mAL).

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
Pheromone drive is not uniform across mAL or P1 populations. ppk23 and
ppk25 channels produce distinct selectivity patterns. The signal flow
model captures multi-hop propagation including network-level interactions,
while path-based analysis traces explicit anatomical routes. Agreement
between models validates the biological signal. P1 subtypes receiving
asymmetric ppk23/ppk25 drive may gate sex-specific courtship behaviors.
